# CW Skate

**A skateboard for Content Warning.** Ride through the Old World, do flips, grabs, grinds and frontflips, chain combos and farm **aura** — and film all of it for views and viewer comments about your skating.

> ⚠️ **Everyone in the lobby needs the mod.** The skateboard is a real item of the game, players without the mod can't see or use it.

## Features

- **Skate 3 style riding:** separate pushes, carving, powerslides, a stance (regular / goofy), curbs and walls that throw you off.
- **Tricks:** ollie, boneless, kickflip / heelflip / shove-it / tre flip / impossible (doubles and triples), spins, 8 grabs, airwalk, superman, **frontflips and backflips**, manuals, kickturns, reverts, hippie jumps, wallrides, wallplants, drop-ins.
- **Grinds** on rails and ledges — and on the **backs of monsters**.
- **Skitching** (grab a running monster or player and get towed) and **skate trains** (hands on the shoulders of the player in front).
- **Combos → aura → ranks** from *Cringe Lord* to **AURA GOD**. At AURA GOD a board smack sends a monster flying, a huge red **L** floats over it… but if it kills you later, you're **HUMBLED** and all your aura is gone.
- **Video content:** tricks on camera give views and **viewer comments about your skating** (English, Russian, French and Chinese), escapes from monsters, bails, smacks…
- **Board wear:** it squeaks, creaks, slows down and finally snaps in half.
- **Board upgrades** in the shop: speed wheels, soft wheels, a maple deck and a **rocket booster** (with fuel).
- **Skate sponsors:** five skate-brand deals on the house monitors — including the **Ride to Hell pro board** and an **insurance policy** that works like a totem of undying.
- **Skins** for your board (your own PNGs), glowing **wheel tracks** in your rank's colour, **grind sparks**, an aura glow, first / third person.
- Everything is synced: other players with the mod see your board, tricks, skin, sparks and fire.
- **Easy / Normal / Hard difficulty:** on Easy the board hops over bumps, curbs and steps by itself and landings forgive mistakes; Normal (default) hops bumps only at pushing speed and forgives a little; Hard is the full Skate 3 experience.
- **Explosive rocket fuel:** smack a fuel canister or a rocket lying on the floor and it blows up (with chain reactions). A rocket burning on your board can blow up in a crash. Both can be switched off.
- **English, Russian, French and Simplified Chinese**, an in-game settings menu (**F8**) and tips on your first ride.
- **Low-end PC mode:** one switch turns off the heavier effects (wheel tracks, aura glow, board shadows, rocket smoke, explosion flashes) and keeps all the skating.

## Getting a board

1. In the shop open **Misc** and order the **Skateboard** ($400).
2. A drone brings it. Pick it up with **E**.
3. Press **B** to get on / off the board.

The board slot not selected = the board hangs on your back. Selected = you hold it, **LMB = smack** with it.
After a bail the board flies off as a normal item — pick it up again with **E**.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **B** | get on / off the board |
| **W** | push |
| **A / D** | turn (the camera follows the board) |
| **S** | brake, at speed — powerslide |
| **Space** (hold → release) | ollie — the longer you hold, the higher |
| **Ctrl** (hold) | manual, **Ctrl + W** nose manual, **Ctrl + A/D** (slow) kickturn |
| **Ctrl + tap Space** | boneless |
| **S + Space** (rolling) | hippie jump — the board rolls on under you |
| **Shift** right after landing | revert |
| **F** (hold) | skitch — grab a nearby monster or player and get towed |
| **Y** (hold) | skate train — both hands on the shoulders of the player in front |
| **Alt** (hold) | rocket booster (if it's on your board) |
| **T** (emote key) | dance on the board while riding |
| **G** (off the board) | throw the board ahead and hop on |
| **F5** | first / third person |
| **H** | the full controls list |
| **Tab** (hold) | records |
| **F8** | settings |

**In the air:** **A/D** spin · **F / G / X / C / Z** kickflip / heelflip / shove-it / tre flip / impossible (again mid-flip = double) · **Shift** grab (+W nosegrab, +S tailgrab, +Ctrl melon, +Ctrl+W crail, +Ctrl+S stalefish) · while grabbing **Space** airwalk, **W+Space** superman · **W+Space / S+Space** frontflip / backflip. Let go of grabs and finish your flips before you land!

**Grind:** ollie onto a rail or a ledge edge. **Wallride:** ollie into a wall at an angle. **Monster grind:** ollie down onto a monster. **Space** pops you off.

## Board upgrades (shop → Misc)

Hold a part and press **LMB** to put it on your board. The parts live in the board itself.

| Part | Price | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Speed Wheels | $35 | +25% top speed, +20% pushing, -20% turning |
| Soft Wheels | $25 | trip on small curbs much less, quieter, a bit slower |
| Maple Deck | $45 | wears out half as fast |
| Rocket Booster | $120 | hold **Alt**: fire, smoke and 110+ km/h. You can barely turn, crashes hurt a lot |
| Rocket Fuel | $10 | refills the rocket |

## Skins

The mod's folder gets a `SkateSkins` folder on the first start with `default.png` (bottom of the board) and `default_grip.png` (top). Copy and rename them (e.g. `fire.png`, `fire_grip.png`), draw your own (top of the picture = nose, 1:4 works best) and pick it in **F8 → Skin**. Other players with the mod see your skin.

## Settings and languages

Everything is in the **F8** menu: physics, keys, bails, aura, sounds, video bonuses and more. `Language` (Auto / Russian / English / French / Chinese) switches the whole mod, `CommentsLanguage` the viewer comments. The viewer comments live in `SkateComments_en.txt` / `SkateComments_ru.txt` / `SkateComments_fr.txt` / `SkateComments_zh.txt` in the mod's folder — add your own lines.

Progress (aura, records) is kept per save file in `BepInEx/config/cwskate_progress.txt`.

Lagging on an older PC? Turn on `PerformanceMode` (F8 → HUD & sound).

## Notes

- Everyone in the lobby needs the mod (see the top). Players with an older version may see things a bit differently.
- If the shop or the viewer comments show squares instead of Chinese characters, switch the game itself to Chinese too: the game loads its Chinese font only then.
- Found a bug? Your `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` says what happened — it helps a lot.
