# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- Skateboard item (shop → Misc, $400), Skate 3 style riding: separate pushes (you see the foot kick the floor), carving, powerslides, regular / goofy stance, the body leans with the board on slopes, curbs and walls throw you off.
- Tricks: ollies, bonelesses, flips (doubles and triples), spins, 8 grabs, airwalk, superman, frontflips and backflips, manuals, kickturns, reverts, hippie jumps, wallrides, wallplants, drop-ins, board throws.
- Grinds on rails and ledges, monster grinds, skitching and skate trains.
- Combos, aura and ranks up to AURA GOD; AURA GOD smacks with a huge red L — and getting HUMBLED.
- Video content: tricks, escapes, bails and smacks on camera give views and viewer comments about your skating.
- Board wear, board upgrades (speed wheels, soft wheels, maple deck, rocket booster with fuel) and board skins.
- Explosive rocket fuel and rocket crashes (both can be switched off).
- Five skate sponsor deals, including the Ride to Hell pro board and an insurance policy that saves you from death once.
- Easy / Normal / Hard difficulty.
- English, Russian, French and Simplified Chinese; F8 settings menu; first-ride tips; records (Tab).
- Low-end PC mode for older computers.
- Everything synced in multiplayer for players with the mod.
